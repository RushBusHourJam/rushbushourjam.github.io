Privacy Policy — Rush Bus Hour Jam

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Rush Bus Hour Jam ("the game") is published by Ilijar Studio. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information. It is short because the game does very little.

What the game stores

Your progress — the level you have reached, your coins, your best chain and your settings — is saved on your device only, in the app's own storage. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and we cannot see it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app erases it permanently.

If you have Android Backup switched on, Google may include that save in your device backup so a new phone can restore it. That backup is governed by Google's privacy policy, not this one.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own, no crash reporting, no chat and no user-generated content. We do not know who you are and we do not receive any information about you.

Advertising

The game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, and device and app identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you are shown and interact with.

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller under Google's privacy and terms and the AdMob policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation entirely, in your device's Google settings under Ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you a consent form the first time it runs, provided through Google's User Messaging Platform. Your choice is stored on your device, and it can be changed at any time from Ad preferences in the game's settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from them. Where required, ad requests are marked as child-directed or age-restricted so that only non-personalised ads are served.

Permissions the app asks for

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to fetch ads. The game itself plays entirely offline.
  • VIBRATE — the short buzz when a move is refused.
  • AD_ID — required by Google Play for apps that use the advertising ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date at the top.
